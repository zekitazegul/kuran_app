# Build 76 verification

The Beyyine failure was reproduced on the user's Wi-Fi SM-S926B with the previous
Android MediaPlayer implementation. Shatri 98:2 completed; opening 98:3 selected
Samsung's SECMidiExtractor with confidence 0.81 and failed with "source has no
track". The source MP3 was complete and matched its catalog hash.

Quran playback now uses Media3 with an explicit MP3 extractor for every reciter
and verse. On-phone logs from build 73 confirm completion of all eight Beyyine
verses, including the previously failing 2-to-3 transition and verse 8 completion
at 15:32:22 device time. No source recording was edited or substituted.

The silent full-corpus audit downloaded and decoded all 31,180 recordings,
checking length and MD5 against the catalog. Each of the five reciters has all
6,236 verses across 114 surahs. Eight outdated hashes were corrected after
independent repeat downloads; two of these also had outdated byte counts:

- Alafasy: 37:152 and 78:40.
- Shatri: 22:52.
- Maher: 6:8, 9:97, 21:70, 21:71 and 29:63.

The corrected records were re-downloaded and decoded. The final report has
31,180 passes and zero failures. This is a file-integrity and decoding audit,
not a linguistic assessment of recitation content. Artifacts:
`build/verification73/full-audio-summary.json`, `full-audio-decode.jsonl` and
`catalog-corrections.json`.

Selection stays visible after release, with independently draggable endpoints
and an action button. Range selection also survives parent rebuilds and does
not compete with reader double-tap or page gestures. Memorization start, end
and repetition fields share a row above the explanation. Widget coverage spans
360x640, 800x360, 800x1280 and 1280x800 in Turkish, English and Dutch. The focused
regression suite passed 60 tests; Dart analysis reported no issues.

Mandatory updates are checked on startup and foreground entry. A discovered
update blocks the reader after download cancellation/failure; a previously
discovered newer version also survives a later network failure. The gate uses
the saved interface locale. In-app updates use Android's standard APK installation
screen and its Open button. The phone blocked background relaunch through both a
package-replaced receiver and a PackageInstaller result PendingIntent. Those
experimental approaches were removed; no additional installer is required for
in-app updates.

The existing standalone first-install bootstrapper was also verified on the phone:
it opened KuranApp, displayed Android's uninstall confirmation for itself, and was
removed after confirmation. KuranApp remained installed and open. The bootstrapper
cannot silently uninstall itself; Android requires that final confirmation.

This intermediate report is superseded by the build 77 phone verification in
`docs/E2E_2026-10-25_build77.md`. The standard Install/Open flow was not separately
signed off during the intermediate build 76 testing.
