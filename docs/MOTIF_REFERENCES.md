# Motif design references

The app renders original vector adaptations of documented Islamic ornamental forms. These are stylistic interpretations, not exact reconstructions or reproductions of a named monument. No museum photograph is bundled in the APK.

- Minbar panels, interlaced polygons, star shapes and split-leaf palmettes: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Panel from Minbar, 91.1.1609, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/444786 ; David Collection, star-shaped minbar/door panel, Cairo c.1300, https://www.davidmus.dk/kunst-fra-islams-verden/trae-elfenben-og-papmache/item/1201?culture=en-us .
- Geometric star tessellation: Victoria and Albert Museum, Islamic tile patterns, https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/design-and-make-your-own-islamic-tile-and-printed-pattern .
- Ottoman floral scrolls and rosettes: Aga Khan Museum, Iznik tiles AKM583/584, related to the Rustem Pasha Mosque repeat-pattern composition, https://collections.agakhanmuseum.org/collection/artifact/tiles-akm583-akm584 .
- Samarkand and Bukhara interlaced octagons, eight-pointed stars and central four-petal rosettes: The Met, tile 1972.88, https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/452379 .
- Samarkand tile mosaic, floral and geometric compositions and bannai brick decoration: Samarkand tourism portal, https://sam.uz/en/culture/koshin-va-mayolika .
- Tashkent regional context: official Uzbekistan tourism portal, Khazrati Imam complex, https://uzbekistan.travel/en/o/kompleks-hazrati-imam/ ; official Tashkent tourism route, https://visit.tashkent.uz/en/popular-route/7 . Tashkent's app motif is a floral tile interpretation, not a claim of copying a specific monument's panel.
- Brick, glazed revetment and carved geometric decorative traditions in Samarkand and Bukhara: UNESCO Silk Roads architectural history, https://en.unesco.org/silkroad/sites/default/files/knowledge-bank-article/vol_V%20silk%20road_architecture%20BIS.pdf .

Design: interlocking star bands and rosettes; repeating rumi stems, leaves and palmettes; cobalt/turquoise Samarkand and Tashkent palettes; warm brick and carved-star Bukhara treatment. Plain remains available. Default opacity is 0.18; user adjustment spans 0.04 to 0.40. Versioned migration raises older faint motifs while retaining subsequent explicit preferences.
